CoCoRaHS
========

Description
  The Community Collaborative Rain, Hail and Snow Network (CoCoRaHS) is a volunteer network of observers who measure and report precipitation from their homes, schools, and workplaces using low-cost manual gauges, typically the recommended 4-inch gauge. The network originated with the Colorado Climate Center at Colorado State University in 1998, following the July 1997 Fort Collins flood, and has since expanded well beyond Colorado. Because observers report from wherever they live rather than from sites selected for a measurement program, the network fills in gaps between traditional observing stations, particularly in areas where precipitation varies sharply over short distances.

  https://www.cocorahs.org

Recommended use
  Verification of quantitative precipitation forecasts and analyses, especially daily accumulation at point locations and in regions where the traditional observing network is sparse

File format
  ASCII (comma-separated values)

Location of data
  https://www.cocorahs.org/ViewData/

Access restrictions
  Freely available to governments, academic institutions, the private sector, participants, and the general public. Unless otherwise noted, CoCoRaHS content and data are released under a `Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>`_, and the network asks that users acknowledge the source when displaying the data.

Spatial resolution, grid, or coverage
  Point observations from the United States, Canada, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, and the Bahamas, with observer density varying widely by region

Temporal resolution
  One daily report covering the previous 24 hours, taken at the observer's regular morning observation time; 7:00 AM local is the preferred time. Supplemental hail, intense precipitation, and multi-day accumulation reports are submitted as events occur.

Starting and/or ending dates
  1998-Present, beginning in Colorado and expanding to additional states and countries in the years that followed

Data latency
  Near real-time; reports become available on the CoCoRaHS website as observers submit them

Variables available
  Daily liquid precipitation accumulation, new snowfall, total snow depth, and snow water equivalent, which is optional but encouraged, plus hail reports
